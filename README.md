# SmartBudget — PLP Startup Pitch Deck

## About the Project

**SmartBudget** is a simple mobile-friendly budgeting solution designed to help students and young people manage their money more effectively.

The project focuses on helping users track income and expenses, create budgets, monitor spending habits, and make better everyday financial decisions.

This repository contains my **PLP Startup Building first-draft pitch deck**, covering the 12 required sections of the assignment.

---

## The 12 Pitch Deck Slides

### 1. Problem

Many students and young people find it difficult to manage their money.

Daily expenses can easily go untracked, and users may spend more than planned or run out of money before the end of the month.

SmartBudget addresses this problem by making personal budgeting simple and easy to understand.

---

### 2. Solution

SmartBudget provides a simple way for users to:

* Record income and expenses
* Create spending budgets
* Categorize expenses
* Monitor their remaining budget
* Understand their spending habits

The goal is to make budgeting accessible to people who are new to financial planning.

---

### 3. Product

SmartBudget is planned as a mobile-friendly budgeting application.

Key features include:

* Income and expense tracking
* Budget creation
* Expense categories
* Spending summaries
* Budget alerts
* Simple financial reports

The first version will focus on the most important features needed to help users manage their daily spending.

---

### 4. Target Market

The initial target users are:

* University students
* College students
* Young professionals
* First-time budgeters
* Young people interested in improving their financial habits

The initial market focus is young people in Kenya, with potential expansion to other African markets in the future.

---

### 5. Market Size

There is an opportunity for digital budgeting tools because many young people use smartphones and digital financial services.

SmartBudget will initially focus on students and young professionals.

The market can potentially expand beyond the initial target group to other individuals who need simple personal budgeting tools.

---

### 6. Competitors

Users currently have several alternatives for managing their money, including:

* Excel and Google Sheets
* General budgeting applications
* Mobile-money transaction histories
* Paper notebooks
* Manual budgeting methods

SmartBudget aims to provide a simpler experience specifically designed for beginner budgeters.

---

### 7. Competitive Advantage

SmartBudget aims to differentiate itself through:

* A simple and beginner-friendly interface
* A mobile-first approach
* Easy expense categorization
* Clear spending summaries
* Features designed around young people's needs
* Potential integration with local payment ecosystems

The focus is on making budgeting easy rather than complicated.

---

### 8. Traction & Roadmap

The project is currently at the **concept and planning stage**.

### Roadmap

**Phase 1:** Conduct user research

**Phase 2:** Build a prototype

**Phase 3:** Test the prototype with students

**Phase 4:** Develop and launch the MVP

**Phase 5:** Collect user feedback and improve the product

Future development will be guided by real user needs and feedback.

---

### 9. Business / Revenue Model

SmartBudget could use a **freemium business model**.

### Free version

* Basic expense tracking
* Basic budgeting
* Spending summaries

### Premium version

* Advanced reports
* Additional budgeting features
* Personalized insights
* Additional financial tools

Future opportunities may also include partnerships with relevant financial-service organizations.

---

### 10. Go-To-Market Strategy

SmartBudget plans to reach its first users through:

* Universities and colleges
* Student communities
* Social media
* Student ambassadors
* WhatsApp communities
* Financial-literacy activities
* Referral programs

The initial goal is to test the product with a small group of users, learn from their feedback, and gradually expand.

---

### 11. Our Ask

At the early stage, SmartBudget is seeking:

* Startup mentorship
* Feedback from potential users
* Technical development support
* Connections with universities and student communities
* Opportunities to test the MVP
* Potential early-stage funding

The support will help transform the idea into a tested and useful product.
